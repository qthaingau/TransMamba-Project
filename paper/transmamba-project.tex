\begin{abstract}
Processing long-context documents remains a primary computational bottleneck for traditional Transformer models due to their quadratic $O(n^2)$ memory complexity. In this work, we present TransMamba-Long, a hybrid architecture combining the sparse global attention mechanism of Longformer with the linear sequence modeling of an 8-layer Causal Mamba decoder. Evaluated on the IMDB dataset with a sequence length of 1024 tokens, our proposed model achieves an outstanding accuracy of 94.58\% and a macro F1-score of 0.9458. Furthermore, the integration of Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT/LoRA) ensures high resource efficiency, maintaining VRAM consumption at approximately 8.5GB, effectively preventing the Out-Of-Memory (OOM) errors typical of standard Transformers.
\end{abstract}
